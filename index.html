Stay on Track

A fully-offline hiking companion that keeps you on your intended trail: a British voice calls out “Left / Right / Straight” at every trail junction, a rising tone warns when you stray off-trail, and OpenStreetMap maps work in airplane mode. SwiftUI, for iPhone and Mac Catalyst.

SideStep

macOS menu-bar sideloader for iOS apps: installs and keeps apps signed with a free Apple ID (zsign, multi-account, auto-refresh). Built on SideStore/AltSign. The companion SideStep Wizard builds one-click iOS-app installers.
