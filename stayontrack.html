Stay on Track

Never again stray from the trail.

A hiking app for iPhone that follows the route you planned and speaks up at every junction: "Left", "Right" or "Straight". Stray off the trail and it tells you how far. Everything is downloaded before you set off, so it works all day in airplane mode with the phone in your pocket.

Four-minute tour

Why it exists

You get lost at the junction

Most hiking apps show you a map and leave you to stare at it. But you rarely get lost in the middle of a trail. You get lost at a fork, where another path branches off and you confidently walk down the wrong one. Stay on Track is built around that moment.

It tells you which way

At each junction a calm British voice says "Left", "Right" or "Straight". Take the wrong one and it says "Wrong turn".

It notices when you stray

Wander 20, 50 or 100 metres off the trail and it says so, then "Back on trail" when you are.

It needs no signal

Maps, trails, elevation and weather are fetched when you load the route. On the trail, airplane mode is fine.

It stays in your pocket

The important cues are spoken, so you can keep your eyes on the path and the phone away.

Before you go

Load a route and see the whole day

Open a GPX file of your planned hike from Files, a share sheet or another app. Stay on Track draws it on a topographic map and works out the rest:

  • Distance, climb and an estimated time, adjusted for the hills (Tobler's hiking function).
  • An elevation profile with the peaks marked, and the forecast temperature for every hour of the walk.
  • Every real junction along the way, from OpenStreetMap, on footpaths and on streets through towns.
  • Cafés and restaurants within 200 m of the route, with how far into the hike you'll reach each one.
  • Shorter or longer alternatives between the same start and finish, in one tap.
  • Navigate to start hands the trailhead to Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, to drive there.

On the trail

Turn-by-turn, for footpaths

Tap Start and walk. A banner at the top shows the next junction and counts down the distance to it; an arrow on the map at each junction points the way to go. As you reach it, you hear it:

The voice is Kokoro, recorded ahead of time and built into the app, so it sounds the same with no signal. Stop at a junction and it repeats the instruction.

Everything else

The details that matter on a long walk

Live stats and two ETAs

Time walked and left, distance, climb, and two finish times: one from the terrain, one from your own pace once you've walked 10 minutes.

A map that reads well

The route thickens on steep ground and fades beyond the next kilometre. Kilometre markers, START and STOP signs, and a faint trail of where you've been.

On the Lock Screen

A Live Activity shows the next junction and its distance on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Never loses your place

If the app is closed mid-hike, it offers to pick up exactly where you were: track, timer and all.

Stops by itself

The hike ends when you reach the finish, or if you speed up to car pace and forget to stop it.

Keeps your track

Your actual walk is recorded and can be exported as GPX to share or keep. Reverse a route to walk it the other way.

Install

Get it onto your iPhone

Stay on Track isn't on the App Store. It's installed with SideStep, a free Mac app that puts apps on your own iPhone with your own Apple ID and keeps them signed. Click the button on a Mac with the iPhone plugged in; if you don't have SideStep yet, it offers an installer that sets it up first.

Prefer another sideloader? Download StayOnTrack.ipa for AltStore, SideStore and similar apps.

A hiking app by John Buckman. SwiftUI, MapKit with offline OpenStreetMap tiles, CoreLocation and AVFoundation. Screenshots show the Dragon’s Back in Hong Kong.
