What it is

A forum built for one community

dcamp is a forum written from scratch for Decent Diaspora, the community of Decent espresso machine owners. It brought over the community's ~8,400 topics and ~115,000 comments from Basecamp, and stays in sync with it. You log in with your existing Decent support account: same email, same password.

Forums and threads

Topics with photos, reactions and boosts, quote and reply, and categories to filter by.

Owners, badged

Each poster shows the machine they own and since when, so you know whose advice comes from experience.

AI summaries

Summarize a forum's last week in one tap, or ask Derek, the forum's assistant, about anything discussed.

In your language

Posts are translated for you, with "Show original" a tap away.

Reading

Forums, threads, conversations

The home screen lists the forums with how many messages each has. Open one for its threads: newest activity first, with a picture, the latest reply and the comment count. Filter to what's popular, what you posted, what you commented on, or where you were mentioned.

Threads are drawn natively, not as a web page: text, photos, links and @mentions, with reactions under each post and new comments appearing live.

Taking part

Post, reply, chat and message

  • A real editor for new topics and replies: formatting, photos from your library or camera, and @mentions that suggest people as you type.
  • Chat, for quick back-and-forth with the whole community.
  • Direct messages, one to one or in groups. On the iPad, the list and the conversation sit side by side.
  • Notifications when someone replies, mentions or messages you, each opening right where it happened.
  • Search across threads, comments, chat and messages, by year, and people.

Anyone with a Decent account can read. Posting is for current Decent machine owners.

iPad and Mac

Room to read

On an iPad, summaries open in a side panel and direct messages show the list and the conversation together. On a Mac, dcamp is a proper Mac app in its own window, signed and notarized by Apple.

Install

Get it onto your iPhone, iPad or Mac

On an iPhone or iPad, dcamp is installed with SideStep, a free Mac app that puts apps on your own devices with your own Apple ID and keeps them signed. Click the button on a Mac with the iPhone or iPad plugged in; if you don't have SideStep yet, it offers an installer that sets it up first.

  • Mac: download dcamp for Mac, unzip it and drag it to Applications.
  • Another sideloader: download dcamp.ipa for AltStore, SideStore and similar apps.
  • No install: use dcamp on the web.
